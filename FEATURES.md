# NGO Management SaaS – Features

## 1. Super Admin (Platform Owner)
- Dashboard: total NGOs, active/expired subscriptions, revenue, usage stats
- NGO (Tenant) Management: view, approve, reject, suspend, activate, delete NGOs
- Subscription Plan Management: create/edit plans (Basic, Standard, Premium), price, duration, limits (users, storage, modules)
- Subscription Approval: approve/reject requests, extend, upgrade/downgrade, trial period
- Billing & Payments: payment gateway, invoices, payment history, refunds, GST/tax
- Platform Staff (Authorized Persons): create sub-admins (Support, Finance, Sales, etc.)
- Roles & Permissions: create roles, assign module-wise permissions (view/add/edit/delete/approve)
- Module Control: enable/disable modules per plan or per NGO
- Support Tickets: view and reply to NGO tickets
- Announcements: send notices/emails to all or selected NGOs
- Global Settings: branding, email/SMS gateway, payment gateway, terms & policies
- Audit Logs: track every action by super admin staff and NGOs
- Reports: revenue, subscription, NGO growth, export to Excel/PDF
- Backup & Security: data backup, login history, 2FA

## 2. Subscription & Onboarding
- NGO registration / sign-up
- Email & mobile OTP verification
- NGO document upload (Registration cert, 12A, 80G, PAN, FCRA) for verification
- Plan selection & online payment
- Free trial
- Auto renewal / expiry reminders
- Feature lock after plan expiry
- Usage limits per plan

## 3. NGO Admin (Organization Owner)
- Dashboard: donations, projects, beneficiaries, volunteers, expenses
- Organization Profile: logo, address, registration details, bank details
- Staff / Authorized Persons: create users (Manager, Accountant, Field Worker, etc.)
- Roles & Permissions: own roles with module-wise access
- Branch / Location Management
- Subscription view: current plan, upgrade, invoices
- Settings: email/SMS templates, receipt format

## 4. Donor & Donation Management
- Donor profiles & history
- Online donation (payment gateway) and offline entry (cash/cheque/UPI)
- Auto 80G receipt generation (PDF) & email
- Recurring donations
- Campaign / fundraising pages
- Donor reports

## 5. Project / Program Management
- Create projects with budget, timeline, location
- Assign staff & volunteers
- Tasks & milestones
- Progress tracking & photo/document upload
- Project-wise fund allocation & utilization

## 6. Beneficiary Management
- Beneficiary registration & profile
- Category (education, health, women, etc.)
- Aid/benefit distribution records
- Beneficiary documents
- Impact tracking

## 7. Volunteer Management
- Volunteer registration & approval
- Skills & availability
- Event/task assignment
- Attendance & hours tracking
- Certificates

## 8. Event Management
- Create events (camps, drives, programs)
- Registration
- Attendance
- Event gallery & reports

## 9. Finance & Accounts
- Income & expense entries
- Fund/grant-wise accounting
- Budget vs actual
- Bank/cash book
- Utilization certificates
- Financial reports (export)

## 10. Grant Management
- Grant/donor agency records
- Proposal tracking
- Grant fund utilization
- Compliance deadlines & reports

## 11. HR / Staff Management
- Employee records
- Attendance & leave
- Payroll (basic)

## 12. Inventory / Assets
- Stock items (food, clothes, medicine kits)
- Stock in/out & distribution
- Asset register

## 13. Communication
- Email / SMS / WhatsApp to donors, volunteers, beneficiaries
- In-app notifications
- Newsletter

## 14. Documents & Compliance
- Document storage
- Expiry reminders (registration, FCRA, 80G)
- Annual report generation

## 15. Reports & Analytics
- Donation, project, beneficiary, finance, volunteer reports
- Charts on dashboard
- Export to Excel / PDF

## 16. NGO Public Website (optional)
- Public NGO page with about, projects, gallery
- Donate button
- Volunteer sign-up form

## 17. Common / Technical
- Multi-tenant architecture (data separated per NGO)
- Role-based access control (RBAC) at both levels
- Secure login, 2FA, password reset
- Activity / audit log
- Mobile responsive
- Multi-language (optional)
- API for mobile app
